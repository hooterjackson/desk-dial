Design proposal · v1 · 22 Sep 2026

Nano_D++
Control Center

One knob, four controls: Sonos volume, Recently Added, Tracks and Windows. The LCD says what you control and what the right-hand button will do. The ring shows position. Buttons light only when they do something.

Direction

Text first on black. Montserrat at the firmware sizes, one ink colour and two greys. White LEDs at low level for position; green only on a lit confirm button, red only on a real Cancel. No sweeps, breathing or halos. Desktop surfaces use Archivo, square corners and 2px rules from Modernist, on a neutral dark ground with no accent red.

Footer slots are icons only, one per button on an even grid. Button 4 uses a verb icon (play, plus, skip, switch) rather than a bare ✓, and the detail line above spells out the consequence.

Assumptions
Bezel may hide the outer 8 px of the LCD. Safe area is a 104 px radius circle.
Sonos reports volume and transport through events; “confirmed” means reported back.
Library items, titles and windows are sample data. Timings in the prototype are simulated.
Existing settings: launch at sign-in, Sonos target, manual IP, Apple Music sign-in. Anything else is tagged Proposed.

Rules for BColour only on ring segments, never on the LCD text. Tracks, bounds, pending and More stay white. Buttons keep their meaning: green confirm, red cancel.

Layout gridLabel 32 · title 50–104 · detail 106–141 · footer icons 154–174 (20 px). Every line is sized to the chord of the safe circle at its height, so text stays 16 px clear of the physical edge.
FooterFour icons centred at x 56, 99, 141, 184: equal spacing, same order as the buttons. No words, so differing label widths can’t make the row look uneven. The old 42/94/146/198 row pushed slots 1 and 4 into the bezel. Empty slots stay empty so the others never shift.

03

Ring, buttons and motion

RecommendedWhite arc
{{ x.n }}

Arc runs clockwise from 7 to 5 o’clock, one segment per 2 %. Body L2, endpoint L3, the two bound marks L1. A pending request shows the confirmed arc at L2 and the requested span at L1, so the endpoint leads and the arc catches up when Sonos reports back.

ExploredGreen → red arc
{{ x.n }}

Adds no information: position already says how far up you are, and the colour repeats it. It also borrows green and red, which mean “confirm” and “cancel” on the buttons, and suggests a danger level that a Sonos percentage cannot measure. Kept as a Tweak for evaluation only.

Ring per mode
Volume — proportional arc with a clear endpoint. 67 detents per turn, so the arc does not track knob angle.
Recently Added, Windows — one L1 landmark per entry, 3 segments apart. 20 detents per turn is 18° per detent, so the cursor moves with the knob exactly. Unavailable and closed entries leave a gap. More is a double landmark.
Tracks — Prev and Next as paired segments 45° either side of a single Neutral mark, matching 8 detents per turn.
Screen change — LCD slides in 16 px and fades (240 ms), from the right when going deeper, from the left when going back. Ring and button lights switch instantly so the cursor never lags; no sweep.
Relative brightness
{{ lv.k }} {{ lv.d }}
Percentages are of maximum LED drive, a starting point for tuning on hardware. On-screen swatches are simulated; real LEDs will look brighter and more saturated.

Supported today Four controls, their haptic profiles and detent counts; 60-segment ring; four RGB buttons; LVGL with Montserrat 12–32; tray app with auto-reconnect and launch at sign-in; Sonos volume, play and skip; Apple Music Recently Added.
Implementation changes this design needs Montserrat 48, digits and % only (~2 KB), for the volume. Fallback: 32 px, which still reads but loses dominance. Glyphs: LVGL built-ins cover all but Windows (uses LV_SYMBOL_COPY) and Switch (LV_SYMBOL_SHUFFLE or a 14 px custom glyph). Companion-owned overlay window, frozen list, focus-loss dismissal. Per-mode bounds and entry index sent to firmware without restarting the profile. Windows app icons: companion reads each window’s icon (WM_GETICON / exe or package icon), scales to 28 × 28 and sends it with the frozen list (≈ 2.3 KB each as RGB565 + alpha); letter tile when missing.
Proposed, not required LED brightness setting (Dim · Standard · Bright). Button test in Settings. Everything else in this document maps to existing behaviour.
