Decisions in this revisionFull list with reasons in the changelog.
32:9 ruleScale by height; everything else is laid out in a centred 16:9 stage. Blur, ambient and dimming extend edge to edge. The two carousels extend sideways on the same pitch, with more cards visible and none shown below 40 % of their prepared size. Up next and the snap tray stay in the stage.
Knob screenTranslate and opacity only, at 60 fps. No scaling; slides are 20 px at most; fades last at least 120 ms. Covers swap instantly. Text shadow is a hard 0 1px 0 at 80 % on top of the darker scrim. Headings may reach r 112 with 0.04 em tracking; mirror labels are RECENT, FAVOURITES and UP NEXT.
Service truthPlay next, Up next and Seek work only while the Sonos queue is playing; elsewhere their buttons dim and a press says why. Like writes the Favorite star. Playlists start with the playable songs; albums are all-or-nothing. Starting shows Starting… on Home with the Working comet.
LEDsWarm #FFBE69 fixed all day; time of day only dims resting brightness. Amber #FF8338 from 80 %, red #FF0000 from 90 % and for failure. Paused Play breathes green. Lists use only their items’ colours. The ring glow uses six fixed levels.
