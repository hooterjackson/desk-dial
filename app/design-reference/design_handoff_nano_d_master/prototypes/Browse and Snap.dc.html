Button grammar1 is Back (Play/Pause on Home). 2 is Open on screen on the knob; on screen, 2 and 3 are a pair (Recently Added / Playlists, Snap left / right) or two modifiers (Shuffle, Like). 4 is the action and the only green button. Each icon has one meaning: heart is always Like, shuffle is always shuffle, expand is always Open on screen. Windows lives only on Home.
One BackButton 1 always goes up one level. From Recently Added, Tracks and Windows that is Home; from the explorer it is Recently Added on the knob. Hold Back for 600 ms to jump Home from anywhere. In Windows, Back also restores focus.
Music explorerThe whole desktop blurs and takes the focused cover’s colour. One detent moves one cover (420 ms ease-out). Buttons 2 and 3 switch between Recently Added and Favourite playlists; each keeps its own position. The knob’s ring and screen mirror the carousel.
SnapButton 2 snaps the highlighted window to the left half, 3 to the right, and the picker stays open for the other side. Once both are filled it closes and Windows keeps them as a Snap group. Closing after one side keeps what you had on the other half. The ring washes that half in the app’s colour.
Up next: verticalTracks are text, not pictures, so they stack vertically where long titles fit. One large cover sits on the left: for an album it stays still while the track numbers move; for a playlist each row has its own cover and the large one crossfades to the focused track. Horizontal stays the pattern for choosing albums.
SeekTracks button 3 turns the knob into a scrubber: 5 s per detent on the fine 67-detent volume profile, so no new haptics. The ring becomes the song, one lap from 12 o’clock, and the screen shows the time at 48 px. Seeks live (sent to Sonos 250 ms after the last detent). Press 3 or Back, or wait 3 s, to return to Prev · Now · Next. Durations here are sample data.
Freed buttons in Tracks2 opens Up next and 3 is Like: one press saves the song to Favourites, which feeds the playlists in the music explorer. Shuffle lives inside Up next, where you can see the new order land. Also considered: Repeat (hold Shuffle), Restart track (double-press Back), a scrub mode, a sleep timer.
CompanionSnap uses SetWindowPos to the monitor work-area halves (restore first if maximized), with DWM extended frame bounds so no gap shows. Explorer artwork is fetched at 600 px and cached; the knob keeps receiving 240 px covers.
