CarouselSelected window at 480 × 300, neighbours at 56 % and 34 %, dimmed with distance; everything stays inside the pane. One detent moves one card: 420 ms ease-out, retargeted mid-flight on fast turns. The list has ends; turning past them nudges 12 px and springs back, matching the ring’s bound.
LabelOnly the centre card is labelled: app, a readable title, and a description parsed from the window title (“Danny Lewis (DM) – Riot Games – Slack” becomes Danny Lewis / Direct message · Riot Games). Minimized windows say so in the description. Neighbours carry only their app icon.
BackgroundLiquid glass: a blurred, saturated pane with a top specular edge; the desktop stays visible. No background: cards float over a 55 % desktop dim, with drop shadows and shadowed text for legibility. Thumbnails here are wireframes; the companion would use live DWM thumbnails.
