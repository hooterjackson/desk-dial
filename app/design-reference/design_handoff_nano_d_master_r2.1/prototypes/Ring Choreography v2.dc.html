Ring choreography v2

Cleaner light, one grammar, new uses. Drag or scroll the knob, ← → turn, 1–4 press. Click a moment to replay it.
Four primitivesSpark (a point that flares), Wave (a front that travels), Wash (a field of colour), Breath (a slow swell). Every moment is built from these.
Three temposQuick 180 ms for touch, 450 ms for answers, 900 ms for state changes. Breath runs at 5.2 s. Nothing modulates brightness faster than 3 Hz.
One voice at a timeA new foreground moment fades the previous one out in 120 ms. The base ducks 35 % under it, so effects read without blowing out.
Light onlyOnly what 60 LEDs can do: no fake rotation or blur. Highlights are tone-mapped with a soft knee, so colours stay saturated and never clip to white.
