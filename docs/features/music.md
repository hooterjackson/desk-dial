# Music

The Music space is where the knob drives your Sonos speaker and your Apple Music library. You reach it from Home by tapping button 1. Turn the knob for volume, browse what you recently added, jump around the queue, seek inside a song, and queue things to play next, all without touching the PC.

Desk Dial is tested on one knob (the author's) with one Sonos household. Everything below describes Desk Dial v2.0.0.

## The buttons

The four buttons sit under the knob's screen, button 1 on the left to button 4 on the right. Their words are always shown at the bottom of the screen, so you never have to remember them.

| Screen | Button 1 | Button 2 | Button 3 | Button 4 | Hold 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Music | Win | Lights | Play / Pause | Knob sets brightness |
| Music | Home | Recent | Tracks | Play / Pause | – |
| Recently Added / Playlists | Back | Full screen | Playlists / Recent | Play | Queue (play next) |
| Full screen (on the PC) | Back | Recent | Playlists | Play | Queue (play next) |
| Tracks (queue) | Back | Up next | Seek | Play | – |
| Tracks (stream) | Back | Up next | Seek | Skip | – |
| Seek | Cancel | Up next | Set | Play (dimmed) | – |
| Up next (on the PC) | Back | Shuffle | Like | Play | Play next |

Two rules apply everywhere in Music:

- **Hold 1 for Home.** Hold button 1 for about half a second (600 ms) and you are back at Home from any screen. You feel a thump when it lands.
- **A dimmed button never acts.** If a button cannot do its job right now, its word is dimmed. Pressing it shows the reason on the knob's screen for two seconds (for example "Nothing loaded" or "Sonos unavailable") and the bottom of the LED ring flashes red.

Buttons 1 and 4 act when you let go, not when you press, so a hold never fires the tap first.

## Volume

On Home and in Music the knob is the volume of the Sonos group your speaker belongs to. One click of the knob (a detent) is 1 %, from 0 to 100. There are walls at both ends: the knob stops, it never wraps around.

The screen shows the level as an arc. While Desk Dial sends a change the status reads "Setting…". If someone else changes the volume (the Sonos app, the speaker's own buttons) the knob follows and says "Changed on Sonos". At the ends it says "Minimum" or "Maximum".

With Settings › Knob › LEDs set to Colour, the LED ring is warm up to 79 %, turns amber from 80 % and red from 90 %, with a flicker of embers at the top. With Warm only the ring stays warm at every level.

## Recently Added and Playlists

Tap 2 in Music to open your Apple Music library's Recently Added list on the knob. Each click moves one item; the cover and its name fill the screen, and the LED ring around the knob shows a short window of items in the colours of their covers, so you see what is coming before it arrives.

<picture><source srcset="../media/music-lists.webp" type="image/webp"><img src="../media/music-lists.gif" alt="Album covers slide past on the knob's screen as it turns; the LED ring shows a band of dots in the covers' colours; a tap on button 3 swaps the list for playlists." width="480"></picture>

- **Turn** to browse. The screen says "3 / 25 · Artist name" as you go.
- **Tap 3** to switch between Recently Added and your favourite playlists. The screen confirms "Favourite playlists" or "Recently Added" and starts at the top of the other list. The button is lit amber while Playlists is shown.
- **Tap 4** to play the item you are on. You feel a thump as you press. The queue is replaced and the knob returns to the Music screen with "Starting…".
- **Hold 4** (one second) to queue the item to play next instead. The knob says "Queued · Album name" once it is in; while Desk Dial is still working it says "Finding songs…" or "Queueing… 3 of 12".
- **Tap 2** for Full screen: the same list opens on your PC as a row of large covers, so you can browse by eye. Button 2 and 3 then pick the list (Recent or Playlists), 4 plays, hold 4 queues, 1 closes it. It also closes on its own after 60 seconds without input.

<picture><source srcset="../media/desktop-explorer-16x9.webp" type="image/webp"><img src="../media/desktop-explorer-16x9.gif" alt="A row of large album covers on a 16:9 monitor, the middle one raised and in focus, sliding sideways as the knob turns." width="480"></picture>

The list reads 25 items at a time and fetches more as you approach the edge. Above 20 items the knob's clicks loosen into a free spin so a long list is quick to cross; smaller lists keep the normal list clicks (20 per turn).

Playlists you have not favourited in Apple Music do not appear. If the list is empty the knob says "No favourites yet · Star one in Music" or "Nothing recently added".

## Tracks

Tap 3 in Music to open Tracks. When Sonos is playing from its queue, Tracks is the whole queue: turn the knob and the screen walks the rows, "Track 7", with the playing row marked and the LED ring showing your position in the queue. Each click is one row, with walls at the first and last.

<picture><source srcset="../media/music-tracks-seek.webp" type="image/webp"><img src="../media/music-tracks-seek.gif" alt="The knob scrolls through numbered rows of the queue, then a tap on button 3 opens Seek: the arc sweeps across the song in 5 second steps." width="480"></picture>

- **Turn** to browse. The screen says "Skip to 9 / 24 · 4 plays" or "Back to 3 / 24 · 4 plays".
- **Tap 4** to jump to the row you are on. The knob nudges in the direction you jumped as you press, Sonos plays it, and the screen says "Playing 9 / 24". On the row that is already playing, 4 is dimmed ("Turn to browse the queue").
- **Tap 2** opens Up next on the PC (below).
- **Tap 3** opens Seek (below).
- **Tap 1** goes back to Music.

When Sonos is playing a stream (radio, AirPlay or line-in) there is no queue to walk. Tracks then becomes a three-position switch: turn left for "Previous", right for "Next", and tap 4 to Skip. The knob rests in the middle ("Turn for previous or next"). Previous or Next is dimmed when the stream does not offer it ("Previous unavailable").

If nothing is loaded on the speaker, Tracks is dimmed in Music and says "Nothing loaded".

## Seek

From Tracks, tap 3 to seek inside the current song. The knob becomes a smooth, clickless scrub: each step is 5 seconds, the arc on the screen shows where you are, and the line below reads "of 4:12 · 3 sets · 1 cancels". Nothing is sent to Sonos while you turn.

- **Tap 3 (Set)** to jump there. The knob says "Jumping…", then "Seek set".
- **Tap 1 (Cancel)** to leave the song where it was: "Seek cancelled".
- **Tap 2** opens Up next and drops the scrub.
- Button 4 is dimmed: "Set or cancel seek first".

You cannot scrub into the last 3 seconds of a song; pushing against the end shows "Stops 3 s before end". If Sonos refuses the jump the knob says "Didn’t jump · try again". Seek is dimmed in Tracks for streams ("Can’t seek · radio", "Can’t seek · AirPlay", "Can’t seek · line-in") and for songs without a known length ("Can’t seek · no length").

## Up next

From Tracks, tap 2 to open Up next on the PC: the queue as a list, with covers, scrolling as you turn the knob. The knob's screen mirrors the row you are on ("5 / 24 · playing" marks the current song).

<picture><source srcset="../media/desktop-upnext-16x9.webp" type="image/webp"><img src="../media/desktop-upnext-16x9.gif" alt="A tall list of songs with small covers on the PC screen; the highlighted row moves down as the knob turns and a heart appears when a song is liked." width="480"></picture>

- **Tap 4 (Play)** plays the row you are on and closes the list; like a jump in Tracks, the knob nudges in the direction you jumped as you press.
- **Hold 4 (Play next)** moves the row to right after the current song: "Plays next · Song title". The playing row cannot be moved ("Already playing").
- **Tap 2 (Shuffle)** shuffles the rest of the queue, or puts it back in order. With 60 or fewer songs left, Desk Dial reorders the queue itself, keeps anything you queued with Play next at the front, and remembers the original order so Shuffle off restores it ("Shuffle off · in order"). With more songs left it switches Sonos's own shuffle mode on instead ("Sonos is shuffling"; the list then shows a "Shuffled by Sonos" card because Sonos does not reveal its order). The button is lit while shuffle is on. The press ticks; nothing more comes when Sonos answers.
- **Tap 3 (Like)** adds the song to your Apple Music favourites: "Liked", and the button lights up. Like only adds. On a song you already like the button is lit and pressing it says "Unfavourite in Music app". Songs that are not in the Apple Music catalogue say "Not an Apple Music song". The press ticks; only a failure adds a buzz.
- **Tap 1** closes the list. It also closes on its own after 60 seconds without input.

For streams Up next is dimmed in Tracks: "Up next is in Music app" (AirPlay), "Radio · no Up next", "Line-in · no Up next".

## Play and Pause

Button 4 on Home and in Music is Play when the speaker is paused and Pause when it is playing. The small icon above the word morphs from one to the other on the knob's screen as the state changes. The knob never guesses: it sends the one action that matches the last state Sonos reported, and while that is in flight the status reads "Starting…" or "Pausing…". A press during an unstable state is ignored silently rather than toggled blindly.

<picture><source srcset="../media/music-playpause.webp" type="image/webp"><img src="../media/music-playpause.gif" alt="A press on button 4 and the play triangle on the knob's screen folds into two pause bars while the status line reads Pausing." width="480"></picture>

## Play next by holding 4

Holding button 4 for a second is the queue action throughout Music: in Recently Added and Playlists, in the full-screen list on the PC, and in Up next. The ring fills while you hold; the landing thumps, the ring lights up, and the knob confirms "Queued · Name" or "Plays next · Song".

<picture><source srcset="../media/music-hold-queue.webp" type="image/webp"><img src="../media/music-hold-queue.gif" alt="Button 4 held on an album cover; the ring fills as a progress arc, then the whole ring lights up and the screen reads Queued with the album name." width="480"></picture>

Play next needs the queue to be the thing playing. When it is not, the knob says why: "AirPlay · use Play", "Radio · use Play", "Line-in · use Play", "Nothing playing · Play", or "Shuffle on · turn it off" when Sonos's own shuffle is on (Sonos would scatter the new songs).

## What needs what

| Feature | Sonos speaker | Apple Music |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Volume, Play / Pause, Tracks, Seek, Skip | Yes | – |
| Up next list, Shuffle, Play next from Up next | Yes | Covers only |
| Recently Added, Playlists, Full screen | Yes | Yes |
| Play and Play next from the lists | Yes | Yes (to find the songs) |
| Like | – | Yes |

Sonos is set up by typing the speaker's address in Settings › Music. Apple Music needs a developer key and an authorisation, described in [Settings](settings.md#music). The only thing Desk Dial ever writes to Apple is a Like.

## Messages on the knob

Every line the knob can show in Music, in one sentence each.

**Status (volume and transport)**

- "Setting…": your volume change is on its way to Sonos.
- "Changed on Sonos": the volume moved from somewhere else; the knob now shows the new level.
- "Minimum" / "Maximum": you are at 0 % or 100 %.
- "Starting…" / "Pausing…": Play or Pause was sent and the knob waits for Sonos to confirm.
- "Paused": the speaker is paused.
- "Playing 3 of 12": an album started but some of its songs were unavailable.
- "Didn’t start": Sonos refused to start what you chose.
- "Album unavailable": the album cannot play because a song in it is unavailable.
- "Nothing loaded": there is nothing on the speaker to show in Tracks.
- "Nothing loaded · pick in Recent": Play has nothing to play; choose something in Recently Added.
- "Nothing playing": Sonos is idle.
- "Sonos unavailable": the speaker does not answer; Windows and Lights still work.
- "Speaker group changed": the Sonos group was changed while an action was in flight; try again.
- "Knob disconnected · reconnect to resume": the PC lost the knob over USB.
- "Home · press 1 again for Music": you tapped 1 right after arriving Home; a second tap opens Music.

**Lists**

- "Loading…": the list is still being read.
- "3 / 25 · Artist": your position and the artist, or "3 / 25 · 14 songs" for a playlist.
- "Recently Added" / "Favourite playlists": the list you just switched to.
- "Nothing recently added · Apple Music library": your Recently Added is empty.
- "No favourites yet · Star one in Music": you have no favourite playlists.
- "Apple Music sign-in expired · Renew on your PC" / "Sign-in expired": authorise Apple Music again in Settings.
- "Library not loaded": Apple Music could not be read; go Home and open the list again.
- "Not available": this item cannot be played.
- "Couldn’t open on screen": the full-screen list could not open on the PC.

**Queueing**

- "Finding songs…": Desk Dial is resolving the songs of what you queued.
- "Queueing… 3 of 12": the songs are going into the Sonos queue.
- "Queued · Name" / "Plays next · Song": done.
- "Queued next": done (older wording, still used in one place).
- "Nothing added · retry": nothing could be queued; Desk Dial removed any partial rows.
- "Partly queued": some rows went in; check the queue in the Sonos app.
- "Song changed · retry": the song changed while queueing; try again.
- "AirPlay · use Play" / "Radio · use Play" / "Line-in · use Play" / "Nothing playing · Play": there is no queue to add to.
- "Shuffle on · turn it off": Sonos's own shuffle is on; turn it off before queueing.

**Tracks and Seek**

- "Turn to browse the queue" / "Press 4 to play": hints while you browse.
- "Skip to 9 / 24 · 4 plays" / "Back to 3 / 24 · 4 plays" / "Playing 9 / 24": where you are and what happened.
- "Turn for previous or next" / "Press 4 to skip" / "Skipping…": the stream switch.
- "Start of queue" / "End of queue": a wall; there is no wrap-around.
- "Previous unavailable" / "Next unavailable": the stream does not offer that skip.
- "Turn to pick previous or next": you pressed Skip in the middle position.
- "of 4:12 · 3 sets · 1 cancels": the song length and what the buttons do in Seek.
- "Jumping…" / "Seek set" / "Seek cancelled" / "Didn’t jump · try again": the seek's outcome.
- "Stops 3 s before end": the scrub cannot go further.
- "Set or cancel seek first": you pressed Play while seeking.
- "Can’t seek · radio" / "Can’t seek · AirPlay" / "Can’t seek · line-in" / "Can’t seek · no length".

**Up next**

- "Loading queue…": the rows are being read.
- "5 / 24 · playing": the current song's row.
- "Shuffle on" / "Shuffle off · in order" / "Sonos is shuffling" / "Shuffling…": the shuffle state.
- "Nothing to shuffle": fewer than two songs are left.
- "Queue changed" / "Didn’t shuffle · try again": the queue moved under the shuffle; try again.
- "Already playing": the playing row cannot be moved to play next.
- "Liked" / "Checking likes…" / "Didn’t save · try again": the Like's outcome.
- "Not an Apple Music song": Like only works on catalogue songs.
- "Unfavourite in Music app": Like only adds; remove favourites in the Apple Music app.
- "Up next is in Music app" / "Radio · no Up next" / "Line-in · no Up next": streams have no queue to show.

**Speaker**

- "Looking for Sonos…": the speaker at the saved address has not answered yet.
- "Sonos unavailable / Looking for Sonos… / Windows still works": the speaker is unreachable; the rest of Desk Dial carries on.

## Limits and known issues

- Play next adds at most the first 100 songs of an album or playlist, in order.
- Only the Sonos queue can be queued into or seeked. AirPlay, radio and line-in cannot take Play next, Seek or Up next; Tracks offers Previous / Next for them when the stream allows it.
- Shuffle has two regimes. With 60 or fewer songs left Desk Dial shuffles the queue itself and can restore the order; with more it turns on Sonos's shuffle mode, whose order Desk Dial cannot show or restore.
- Like only adds a favourite. Unliking is done in the Apple Music app.
- Playlists shows only playlists you have favourited in Apple Music.
- Sonos is polled once a second, so a change made in the Sonos app can take up to a second to reach the knob.
- Desk Dial controls one speaker's group, chosen by address. There is no speaker discovery and no room picker.
- Album covers on the knob need Settings › Music › Album artwork On and are cached in memory only.
